Our Story

Building victorious lives through character and God's Word

Vision & Mission

Life is Character Radio is a dynamic, faith-based broadcast program that empowers listeners to live victorious, character-driven lives. We use the powerful metaphor of "life as a script" to help people understand their roles and find victory through God's Word.

Our program discusses life's real challenges - failure, conflict, temptation, loss, and uncertainty - and demonstrates how people can win through character, faith, and spirit-filled living.

"Life is a Script. Win with Character."

Host: Paul Kumeni Agbo

Passionate about empowering people to discover their God-given potential and live lives of purpose, character, and victory through faith. Paul brings years of ministry experience and a heart for transformation to every broadcast.

Through Life is Character Radio, Paul shares practical wisdom and biblical insights that help listeners navigate life's challenges with confidence, character, and unwavering faith.

Why Choose Life is Character Radio?

Scripture-Rooted Wisdom

Practical strategies anchored in God's Word for tackling real-life challenges with biblical principles.

Character-Driven Victory

Empowering listeners to live victorious lives through character development and spiritual growth.

Hope & Encouragement

Bringing hope, encouragement, and practical guidance for every stage of life's journey.

Who We Serve

We serve young adults to middle-aged adults (18-45) who are seeking spiritual growth, empowerment, and practical answers to life's challenges.

Our audience is socially engaged, mobile-first, and values authentic, visually compelling content that speaks to real-life experiences with biblical truth.
